A little about me

I’ve always wanted to know how things work.

It started with video games, my first websites, and running gaming servers with friends. I loved the challenge of figuring things out for myself.

I began my career in tech support but knew I wanted to write software. Choosing to leave that comfort zone opened the door to development, and eventually to becoming a Technical Architect. I still enjoy learning just as much as I did back then.

01

Get to the root of it. I genuinely enjoy the difficult bugs—the kind that make you trace a problem across code, servers, databases, and everything in between.

02

Give people room to grow. I like helping developers find their own answers. When something goes wrong, I’d rather fix it and learn together than look for someone to blame.

03

Get comfortable being uncomfortable. Whether it’s a new language, a 3D printer, or a completely different hobby, I believe you grow by trying what you don’t know yet.

Featured independent product · In development

ISSF Trainer

A mobile training companion I’m building for ISSF pistol athletes. It brings together structured live-fire, dry-fire, and eye-training exercises with Call Your Shot, practice scoring, and training history.

Built around 25m Standard Pistol, 25m Sport Pistol, and 10m Air Pistol. Still in development, with a focus on making competition practice easier to understand and more accessible.
