Danny Luu / ISSF Trainer

Independent product · In development

Practice with purpose.

ISSF Trainer is an independent mobile companion for Olympic-style target sport, bringing competition education, scoring tools and session records into one place.

ISSF Trainer / Focus · Timing · Consistency

Designed for meaningful practice

Tools built around the sport.

The app brings together utilities for understanding competition formats, keeping score and organizing practice records.

01 / TRAIN

Structured sessions

Practice with competition-oriented timing and guided exercises designed for ISSF pistol disciplines.

02 / REFLECT

Call Your Shot

Record where a shot felt like it landed, compare it with the result, and build awareness over time.

03 / SCORE

Scoring tools

Work through competition scores and training results with convenient, focused calculators.

04 / LEARN

Rules & disciplines

Find approachable explanations of formats, scoring, equipment, and key competition concepts.

05 / REVIEW

Practice history

Keep track of relevant training sessions and revisit your progress and observations.

06 / FOCUS

Built for athletes

A clear, mobile-first experience intended to make deliberate practice accessible at different experience levels.

Why it exists

Why I started building it.

I started this project as a software engineer exploring a sport that has its own rules, terminology and competition formats.

ISSF Trainer is my attempt to put the information and record-keeping tools I wanted in one mobile experience. It is an independent personal project, not an official ISSF product.
