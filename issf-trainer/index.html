ISSF Trainer / Focus · Timing · Consistency

Designed for meaningful practice

Tools built around the sport.

From learning the format to reviewing each session, ISSF Trainer brings practical training utilities together in one place. Capabilities may evolve during development.

01 / TRAIN

Structured sessions

Practice with competition-oriented timing and guided exercises designed for ISSF pistol disciplines.

02 / REFLECT

Call Your Shot

Record where a shot felt like it landed, compare it with the result, and build awareness over time.

03 / SCORE

Scoring tools

Work through competition scores and training results with convenient, focused calculators.

04 / LEARN

Rules & disciplines

Find approachable explanations of formats, scoring, equipment, and key competition concepts.

05 / REVIEW

Practice history

Keep track of relevant training sessions and revisit your progress and observations.

06 / FOCUS

Built for athletes

A clear, mobile-first experience intended to make deliberate practice accessible at different experience levels.

Why it exists

A better way to prepare.

Sport shooting rewards focus, consistency, and a willingness to learn. Practice should have tools that reflect those values.

ISSF Trainer is a personal project by Danny Luu, combining software engineering with a growing interest in competitive target shooting. The goal is a useful, approachable companion for athletes learning and training in ISSF pistol events.

Built for learning, deliberate practice, and sport competition. The focus is on making training clearer and more approachable for athletes.
