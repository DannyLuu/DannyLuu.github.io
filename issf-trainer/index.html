Featured work / ISSF Trainer

ISSF Trainer

Designing a mobile app for a specialist sport.

An independent Flutter project for athletes in Olympic-style precision pistol sport, shaped by the references and records I wanted in one place when I took up competition.

In development

ISSF Trainer Reference Scoring Session history

The starting point

Too much to piece together.

When I got into ISSF Standard Pistol competition, I found myself keeping track of the sport’s formats, scores, and notes in different places.

I wanted a single mobile app where an athlete could look up a competition format, record a score, and revisit a past session. Each task is small. The product work is figuring out how they fit together without making people learn a whole new vocabulary.

Product design

Three jobs for one app.

Reference material, recording a result, and reviewing a session each call for a different kind of screen. The app’s scope is organized around those tasks.

Find a reference

Use recognizable discipline names and short explanations so an athlete can find the relevant competition information without starting from a long manual.

Record a score

Keep score entry focused on the result being recorded. The interface needs to make it easy to notice when an entry is missing or in the wrong place.

Revisit a session

Keep past entries available with enough context that a result still makes sense when an athlete comes back to it later.

Engineering considerations

What shaped the design.

A shared mobile interface

I chose Flutter for the app. It lets me work on a shared mobile interface while I refine the screens, navigation, and interactions around each task.

Domain rules and screen design

Competition terminology belongs in the product because athletes recognize it. The design challenge is deciding which distinctions a screen needs to show, and which can sit in the reference material until someone needs them.

What I’m learning

Knowing the sport helps me spot requirements. Designing the app forces me to explain them to someone who may only be looking for one answer or trying to record one result.

Project status

Still in development.

I’m continuing to refine the mobile experience as an independent project. The design areas above describe the scope I’m working toward.
